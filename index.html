Who am I?

Michael Pearce?

My name is Michael Pearce. I’m a Senior SRE and Cloud Infrastructure Engineer with 25+ years of experience keeping complex systems reliable, secure, and automated. My work spans the full stack of modern infrastructure — Azure, Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code with Terraform and Ansible, through to F5 load balancing, API management, and deep observability with tools like Dynatrace, Prometheus, and Grafana.

I am taking classes for Software Engineering to improve my skills in my everyday work.

I also work with Non-Profit organizations like the Women Marines Association and the Loyal Escorts of the Green Garter.
